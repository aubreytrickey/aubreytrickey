Dillard's

My two semesters as a Dillard's Campus Collective Ambassador have provided me with experience in partnership marketing, content creation, and brand promotion. Through organizing partnership activations from planning to execution, I have gained professional interpersonal and collaborative skills that I look forward to bringing into my future career.

Dillard's x Mizzou Softball

The goal of Dillard’s x Mizzou Softball was to allow each athlete to represent their individualized style and personality, beyond being SEC athletes. Generating over 43,000 views, this collaboration showcased their confidence in their own style and displayed the variety of apparel Dillard’s carries.

Dillard's x Alpha Phi

The intention behind Dillard’s x Mizzou Alpha Phi was to exhibit the true feeling of a sisterhood. This shoot aimed for a playful, candid aesthetic filled with warmth, whimsy, and genuine affection. The goal is to feel the friendship and laughter through the screen, while embracing a youthful and feminine feel.

Dillard's x Mizzou Golden Girls

The concept of Dillard’s x Mizzou Golden Girls was to showcase the versatility of Dillard’s Fall 2026 collection while highlighting the genuine friendship and connection between the girls. This shoot aimed for styling that felt elevated, but the overall presence to remain candid and authentic. The creative direction allowed personality while still remaining cohesive, celebrating individual style and emphasizing connection.
