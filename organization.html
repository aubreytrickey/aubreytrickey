Social Media Execution

Healium is a virtual reality company based in Columbia, Missouri. The company’s goal is for consumers to achieve a “state of calm” through a scientific approach of self management. Healium is currently seeking rebranding, emphasizing the relaxation aspect of their product. Within this, the company is striving for a cleaner, lighter aesthetic, while maintaining their brand colors. This is shown within the re-design by focusing on cool-tones, peaceful style, and innovative design while maintaining brand values.
