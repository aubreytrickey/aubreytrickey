About Aubrey:

Hi! I’m Aubrey, a 21-year-old emerging fashion marketer from Kansas City, Missouri. Growing up, I’ve always had a passion for creating. From casual doodles to carefully curated feeds & imagery, I have loved the art of creative expression for as long as I can remember. Now, as a senior at the University of Missouri studying Strategic Communication & Textile and Apparel Management, that passion has turned into a reality. By combining my interests in writing, advertising, & fashion, I have been able to hone in on my skills of visual storytelling, brand identity, trend forecasting, & aesthetic design. My experiences range from partnership marketing, pitching advertising campaigns, designing mock-ups of products, and content creation.
